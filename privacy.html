Privacy

The short version: we keep only what is needed to save your progress, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

You do not need an account

Every course works without signing in. If you don't sign in, your progress (completed lessons, best quiz scores) and your light or dark theme choice are saved only in your own browser. We never receive them.

If you sign in

Signing in lets your progress follow you between devices. When you sign in we store:

  • your email address (and your name, if you sign in with Google and it is shared with us), to identify your account;
  • your progress: which lessons you completed and your best quiz score for each.

That is all. We do not store your quiz answers, what you read, or anything about your browsing.

Where it is kept, and who can see it

Account data is stored with our database and sign-in provider, Supabase, in a data center in Mumbai, India. Access is restricted by database rules so that each signed-in learner can only read and change their own progress. If you choose Google sign-in, Google tells us your email address and name; we do not receive your password or other Google data.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies. The only things kept in your browser are your progress, your theme choice and (when signed in) your sign-in session.

Deleting your data

Choose Delete my account… from your account menu (top right after signing in). This permanently removes your account and all your saved progress from our servers straight away. To remove progress saved only in your browser, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Signing out on a shared computer

When you sign out, the progress belonging to your account is also removed from that browser, so the next person does not see it. It stays safely in your account.
